A chat assistant that learns from your docs and answers like it's read them.

Assistance is a tiny, fast RAG chatbot you can train on your own knowledge base. Drop in your documentation, approve the answers, and your users get an assistant that knows your product better than you do — without sending a single byte to a third-party model.
